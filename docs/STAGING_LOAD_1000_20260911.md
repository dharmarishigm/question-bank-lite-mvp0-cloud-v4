# Staging 1,000-student load attempt — 11 September 2026

Target: qb-security-staging-00002-fl9. Execution: qb-security-staging-load1000-xtf56.
Production and staging service configuration were unchanged. A separate bounded one-task load generator (1 CPU, 1 GiB, 900-second timeout, no retries) was used.

## Result: stopped during ramp-up

The test peaked at 464 active exam students and stopped automatically after three HTTP 429 responses. There were 1,832 successful requests; no attempts completed before abort. All 1,000 temporary sessions were revoked, test users disabled, and dummy exam 3 closed by the cleanup block.

The intended workload was 1,000 distinct sessions ramping over 120 seconds, each taking a 10-minute, 20-question exam with saves and reads about every 29 seconds. The existing ingress rate limit is 1,200 requests/minute per perceived client IP. Requests from this single load generator encountered that protection. This is not a successful 1,000-user capacity validation or proof of a 464-user capacity ceiling. Start latency also rose (p95 3.9 seconds), which warrants investigation before larger testing.

No rate limit was increased or bypassed. A follow-up capacity test requires a reviewed distributed load-generator design and validation of trusted client-IP handling. Users behind shared school/office NATs may also be affected by aggregate per-IP limits. Investigate in staging before changing policy.
