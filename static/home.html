Q. A body accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 5 s. What is the acceleration?

Your answer2 m/s²
Correct answer4 m/s²
Explanation

Use v = u + at with u = 0, v = 20 m/s and t = 5 s, so a = (20 − 0) / 5 = 4 m/s². Topic: Kinematics · Difficulty: Easy.
