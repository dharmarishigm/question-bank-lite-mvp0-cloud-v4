The full exam lifecycle

Everything the exam needs

One workflow covers the whole examination — before candidates arrive, while they attempt, and after they submit.

Before the exam

  • Invite candidates with a registration link
  • Candidates sign in with Google or register directly
  • Schedule the window, duration and attempts allowed
  • Track who has registered and who is still pending

During the exam

  • Timed attempts with a server-controlled clock
  • Access limited to invited, verified candidates
  • AI-assisted proctoring flags irregular activity
  • Answers saved continuously, so a lost connection is not a lost attempt

After the exam

  • Automatic scoring, including negative marking
  • Results released to candidates when you choose
  • Candidates review each answer with worked solutions
  • Administrators review any attempt in full detail

Built for examination teams

Why teams choose MeritIQra

Run exams anywhere

Candidates attempt from their own device in a browser. No exam-hall booking, no software rollout, no local IT project.

Integrity you can evidence

Every attempt keeps an auditable record of activity, so an integrity decision rests on evidence rather than on suspicion.

Results without the wait

Attempts are scored on submission and released under your control, cutting the gap between the last candidate and the published result.

Scales with the intake

The same setup serves a single classroom test and a large recruitment or certification drive, with capacity handled for you.

Clear roles and permissions

Administrators, proctors and candidates each see only what their role allows, so exam content stays with the people who own it.

One platform end to end

Registration, delivery, scoring and result review live in one place, so nothing has to be reconciled between separate tools.
